S&S Automations

S&S provides workflow automation services. My job was to make the business value of that easy to see for the people running the operation. I didn't build or implement any of the automation. I explained why it matters.

Some details and examples stay private, so this page shows how I think rather than internal files.

The challenge

Make the value of workflow automation understandable and relevant to business operators.

Nobody wakes up wanting to automate something. They want the Tuesday backlog to disappear. So the content had to start with the work people already know is eating their day.

The framework behind the content

1

Find the work

Spot the repetitive tasks quietly eating a team's day.

2

Show the cost

Put that time and effort into terms an operator cares about.

3

Show what changed

Explain what the business gets back once the work is automated.
